Stone bridges tell a lot
Torch near a stone bridge means a deadend on that side, but only going straight, not guaranteed deadend moving sideways.

You can see a squares of water on the turns and crossroads
Those sqares mean the river turns, with the square facing the turn direction
If sqare is trying to tell you the river turns backwards, it mostly means it's a crossroad and you can either continue going right or turn up
There's only one tile that goes straight while having a square on it, and that square is facing back anyway

Another way to tell it's a turn are washed up boats. There's one boat turn on the way left, and one on the way right. All other boats belong to the exit tiles.

When you go up to the left, expect to turn right and keep to the right side. Memorize the rules for the crossroads and camps, but in most cases you will just turn right.
For sorcs: River on turns goes either in a very low arc or very big arc, but nothing close to the sqare (talking about inner arcs here). Whenever you see river sharp to the right it's a crossroad.
It applies for the way down as well, whenever rivers inner arc turns in 0-1 jumps it's a crossroad. It's closer to a circle or a bubble for the turns.

Any bubble-like (square without one corner) river turn is some sort of a turn. If you see it going straight it's a crossroad with a turn. If it's on the way left/right it means it's 100% a turn.
