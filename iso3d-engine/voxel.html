Export a blank, labeled atlas image matching this material's own face layout, paint the real art into it externally, then re-upload the finished image below as one whole-atlas image instead of uploading per-face images above.
Gasses are a distinct class from ordinary blocks — no texture, no cube geometry at all, just a soft volumetric tint (the same fog-shader technique as vertical fog). Every image/shape/autotile field above is ignored while this is checked.
Weight 0 = expands evenly in every direction. Negative = rises (steam, smoke). Positive = sinks and pools along the floor (heavy gas, like chlorine or CO2). Wind (the HUD dropdown) still applies on top either way, not replaced by weight.

Billboard Maker

A billboard is a transparent-PNG sprite that always faces the camera, standing upright on the voxel it's placed in — trees, bushes, clutter. Different from Object Maker (fixed 3D box geometry): this is a single flat image, alpha-cutout, that spins to face you as the camera orbits. Click a library entry's name to select it for placement, "edit" to load it back into the form below, then (in billboard mode) left-click a cell's top to place it, alt+click to remove.
Detected % transparent padding at the image's bottom —
Moves the visible art only (positive = raise, negative = slight plant). Shadow contact is pinned to the ground automatically — you don't need to sink the texture under the grass to close a shadow gap (that just clips the roots).

Spell Maker

A spell is a named effect + radius, cast by clicking the map (in spell-cast mode). "Gust of Wind" is just one pre-built example (clear gas, radius 3) — make your own with a bigger/smaller radius, or a different effect entirely.
Damages blocks with HP: grass/dirt/sand/mud/wood/planks/glass/shelf (and custom materials you set HP on). Stone, bedrock, cliffs, dungeon walls are indestructible. Fire vulnerability doubles fire damage on grass/wood.
FX recipe (Disgaea-style, optional)
Leave blank for smart defaults from effect/name. Multi-hit + bolt + pillars.
